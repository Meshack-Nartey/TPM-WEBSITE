    What Partnering with Missions Means

    Partnering with Missions means sending the Gospel beyond our local walls. TPM is committed to reaching communities, towns, and regions that still need a genuine encounter with Jesus Christ — through evangelism, branch planting, and outreach efforts.

    Your support helps cover the cost of travel, resources, logistics, and ground-level ministry work. Every cedi given to Missions is a seed sown into souls — people who will encounter Christ because someone chose to give.
