Worker Groups

Every member of our church body has gifts and talents. Discover where you can serve and make a meaningful impact in God's Kingdom. Join one of our dedicated worker groups and use your gifts to build up the Body of Christ.

Communion Stewards

Communion Stewards

Serves with deep reverence in the preparation and administration of the Lord's Supper, upholding the sanctity of one of the Church's most sacred practices.

Ushering

The first point of contact for members and visitors — warm, professional, and attentive to seating and order throughout the service.

Protocol

Coordinates ministers, guests, and leadership at every special service and programme, upholding the honour of God's house with precision.

Hospitality and Welfare

Cares for members and guests in need — welfare support, refreshments, and visiting the sick — God's hands extended in practical love.

Pure Word

Supports discipleship and Bible study so sound doctrine stays the foundation of everything at TPM.

Media and Publicity

Handles the visual, digital, and communication needs of the ministry — flyers, social media, recording, and content that carries the message beyond the building.

Music

Leads the congregation into God's presence through anointed worship — instrumentalists, vocalists, and choir together.

Theatre and Arts

Brings biblical stories and spiritual truths to life through drama, dance, and mime.

Finance

Faithful stewards of the church's resources — offerings, records, and allocation, with integrity and transparency.

Organizing

The backbone of every TPM event — planning, logistics, and scheduling so each gathering runs with order and purpose.

Sounds and Technical

Runs the audio, lighting, projection, and livestream — mostly behind the scenes, always critical to the service.

Growth

Follows up with new converts and connects visitors into the church community, so no one falls through the cracks.

Literature

Distributes the ministry's books and devotionals — including Apostle Andrews A. Ofori's own — to members and the wider public.

Miscellaneous

A versatile team that steps in wherever there's a need — always ready, always available, for God's house.

The Pastor's Office

Provides administrative and pastoral support to TPM's leadership — scheduling, correspondence, and day-to-day coordination.
