    What Partnering with the Building Project Means

    The Church Building Project is about raising a permanent, God-honouring home for Transformation Project Ministries. A dedicated building means more room for worship services, leadership training, prayer meetings, counselling, and the raising of future ministers.

    This is not just bricks and mortar — it is an investment into a lasting legacy. When you partner with the building project, your name is written into the foundation of a house that will serve generations to come.
